{# The Scan button, in its own file because two responses have to render it. It sits in the Buyout Analyzer but its presence is a fact about the TEAM PANEL: the scan's out-of-band swaps target `bo-` dot placeholders that team_panel.html renders for BOT only, so on an opponent every swap missed and htmx logged htmx:oobErrorNoTarget — a button that did nothing but fill the console. Since /team-view swaps only #team-panel, gating it inside the buyout panel alone left the button missing after you switched BACK to your own team, until some unrelated full-page swap happened to restore it. So /team-view returns this fragment out-of-band as well (`scan_oob`). The wrapper div is unconditional: it is the OOB swap's target, and a target that disappears with its contents can only be swapped in one direction. Gated on a boolean rather than on viewed_team, which only team_panel.html may read: the question here is whether the swap targets are in the document, not whose roster it is. #}
